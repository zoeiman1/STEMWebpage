Program Overview

Empowering the Next Generation of CS Leaders

The Tuskegee University S-STEM program integrates academic scholarship, high-performance research laboratories, and institutional retention interventions. Scholars engage in foundational computer science inquiry, quantum computing, and educational outreach to sustain academic momentum and build competitive portfolios for graduate school and tech industries.

Faculty Mentored Research

Undergraduate apprenticeships under Dr. Xiao Chang exploring quantum machine learning (QCNN, QLSTM, Quantum Autoencoder), groundwater forecasting, and network security.

Peer Coding Seminars

Upper-level S-STEM scholars design and lead introductory programming workshops for incoming CS freshmen, reinforcing mastery and driving retention across the department.

Career & Conference Travel

Travel funding enabling scholars to present research nationwide at AAAS S-STEM, ERN, NSBP-NSHP, AAAI, and IBM-HBCU Quantum symposia with internship debriefs and career mentoring.
